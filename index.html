What We Study

Research Interests

We combine molecular, cellular, and systems approaches to understand the neural basis of perception, plasticity, and cognition.

🧠

Synaptic Plasticity & Memory

We study how synaptic connections are modified during learning, focusing on the molecular cascades that convert short-term neural activity into lasting structural changes.

LTP / LTD Hippocampus NMDA receptors
🔬

Cortical Circuit Dynamics

Using large-scale electrophysiology and calcium imaging, we map how populations of neurons in the cortex encode and transform sensory information in real time.

In vivo imaging Electrophysiology Neural coding
💡

Optogenetic Dissection of Circuits

We leverage optogenetics to selectively activate or silence defined neuronal populations, establishing causal links between circuit activity and behavior.

Optogenetics Chemogenetics Behavior
🧬

Transcriptomics of Neural States

Single-cell RNA sequencing and spatial transcriptomics allow us to characterize how gene expression programs shape neuronal identity and plasticity across brain regions.

scRNA-seq Spatial transcriptomics Cell types
📊

Computational Neuroscience

We develop and apply computational models to interpret large neural datasets, linking theoretical frameworks to experimental data from behaving animals.

Neural manifolds Dimensionality reduction Modeling
🐭

Disease Models

We collaborate with clinical teams to validate circuit-level findings in rodent models of neurological disorders, including Alzheimer's disease and epilepsy.

Alzheimer's Epilepsy Translational
